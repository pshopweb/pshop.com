Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data PShop collects, why we collect it, and the control you have over it.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run PShop well:

Information you give us

  • Account details — your name, email address, mobile number and password.
  • Delivery information — shipping addresses, recipient names and contact numbers.
  • Order history — the products you buy, returns you raise and reviews you write.
  • Communications — messages you send to our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser data — type, operating system and screen size, used to render the site correctly.
  • Usage data — pages viewed and products browsed, used to power "recently viewed" and recommendations.
  • Approximate location — derived from your delivery pincode to estimate shipping times.

What we never collect

PShop does not store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Payment credentials go directly to our PCI-DSS compliant payment partners and never touch our servers.

How we use your information

  • To process orders, arrange delivery and handle returns or refunds.
  • To send transactional updates about your orders by email, SMS or push notification.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • To personalise product recommendations and search results.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations in India.

We send marketing messages only if you have opted in. You can opt out any time from Settings → Notifications or the unsubscribe link in any email.

Cookies and local storage

PShop stores your cart, wishlist, theme preference and browsing history in your browser's local storage. This keeps the site fast and works even offline.

  • Essential — session, cart and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference — dark mode, language and layout choices.
  • Analytics — anonymous usage statistics to improve the site.

Clear this data any time from Settings → Data and storage or your browser settings.

How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted SHA-256 hashes — never in plain text.
  • Session tokens are cryptographically signed and expire automatically.
  • Access to customer data is restricted to staff who need it, and is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever detect a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • Access — download all your data from Settings → Export.
  • Correct — update your details from Edit Profile.
  • Delete — remove your account from Settings → Danger Zone.
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of marketing at any time.
  • Complain — escalate to our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. After deletion:

  • Profile, addresses, cart and wishlist are removed immediately.
  • Order and invoice records are retained for 8 years as required by Indian tax law.
  • Anonymised analytics may be kept indefinitely — these cannot identify you.

Children's privacy

PShop is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it promptly.
