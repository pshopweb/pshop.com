Par ek zaroori baat — storage ≠ speed

Ye samajhna important hai: Drive me jagah hone ka matlab ye nahi ki website tez chalegi. Agar listing page par 12 products hain aur har image 1600px ki hai, to mobile user ko 4 MB download karna padega — sirf thumbnails dekhne ke liye.

Isliye maine Google ka free image CDN use kiya hai:

KahanURLSize
Listing cards.../ABC=w400~30 KB
Tablet / retina.../ABC=w800~90 KB
Product page, zoom.../ABC~320 KB

=w400 lagane se Google khud chhoti copy bana kar deta hai — bilkul free, koi extra storage nahi. Ek hi image se teen size mil jate hain.

Result: listing page 4 MB ki jagah 360 KB load karta hai — 11× tez.

Ye apne aap hota hai. Product save karte waqt thumb field me =w400 wala URL chala jata hai, aur images[] me full-size rehti hai zoom ke liye.

PNG transparency ko white background milta hai, warna JPEG me kaala aa jata.

Backend me kya better hua

apiUploadImage() ab:

  • File type check — sirf JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF. PDF bheja to saaf error
  • Size limit — 5 MB. Badi image par: "Image bahut badi hai (6.4 MB)"
  • Filename sanitize — special characters hata deta hai
  • Drive subfolders — PShop Uploads/Products, /Avatars — saaf organized
  • Behtar URL — lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/ID (ye <img> me hamesha

chalta hai; purana uc?export=view kabhi-kabhi block ho jata tha)

Naya endpoint uploadImages — ek call me kai images, aur agar koi fail ho to baaki phir bhi upload ho jati hain.

3️⃣ Dates ab achhi dikhti hain

Pehle sab jagah ek jaisa boring format tha. Ab context ke hisab se:

KahanPehleAb
Order timeline25 Jul 2026, 04:30 pmToday, 04:30 pm
Kal ka order24 Jul 2026, 11:00 amYesterday, 11:00 am
Delivery date28 Jul 2026Monday, 28 Jul
Kal aa raha26 Jul 2026Tomorrow
Purana order15 Dec 2025, 09:00 am15 Dec 2025

Do naye helpers:

fmtSmart(date) — Today / Yesterday / Tomorrow, phir "25 Jul, 04:30 pm", purana ho to saal ke saath.

fmtDelivery(date) — "Today" / "Tomorrow" / "Monday, 28 Jul". Ek hafte se aage ho to "Sat, 02 Aug".

Ye order timeline, track order aur payment success page par lage hain.

Saare date functions ab invalid date par crash nahi karte — — dikhate hain.
