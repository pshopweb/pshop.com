We make online shopping simple and honest

PShop started in 2021 with one idea: every Indian household deserves genuine products at fair prices, delivered on time — without the confusion.

Our story

PShop began in a small Bengaluru apartment when our founders spent three weeks trying to buy a genuine phone charger online. Every listing looked identical, reviews were unreliable, and the product that arrived was a counterfeit.

That frustration became our mission. We built PShop around a simple promise: if we list it, we stand behind it. Every seller on our platform is brand-authorised, every product passes a quality check before dispatch, and every price you see is the price you pay — no hidden charges at checkout.

Today we serve customers across 19,000+ pincodes, from metros to small towns, with a catalogue spanning electronics, fashion, home essentials, beauty and more.
